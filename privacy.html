Reels Blocker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Reels Blocker is an iOS app with a Safari extension that hides short-video sections (Reels, Shorts, For You feeds) on social-media websites. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

What the app stores

  • Your settings (which sections to hide) and blocking counts used for the "time saved" estimate. These stay on your device, inside the app's own storage, and are never sent to us.
  • Subscription status, provided by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

What the extension does on websites

The extension runs only on the sites you allow in Safari settings. It hides page elements locally. It does not read, store or transmit the content of pages, your account, messages or browsing history.

Network requests

Once a day the extension downloads an updated list of hiding rules (a small JSON file) from yonghostra.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP address of that request under GitHub's privacy statement. No identifier about you is included in the request.

Analytics and ads

None. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no third-party trackers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
